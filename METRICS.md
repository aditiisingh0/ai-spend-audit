# Metrics

## North Star Metric

**Qualified leads generated per week** — defined as a user who:
1. Completed a full audit
2. Submitted their email
3. Has identified savings > $100/month

Why this and not DAU or total visits: This tool is used once per quarter,
not daily. DAU is meaningless. What matters is whether the tool is
generating warm leads for Credex — users who have seen real savings
numbers and left their contact details.

## 3 Input Metrics That Drive the North Star

**1. Audit completion rate**
Definition: Users who click Run Audit ÷ users who load the page
Target: > 35%
Why it matters: If people land and leave without running an audit,
the top of funnel is broken — bad copy, confusing form, or wrong audience.

**2. Email capture rate**
Definition: Users who submit email ÷ users who see results page
Target: > 20%
Why it matters: Audit completion without email capture = anonymous
traffic with no lead value. This is the money metric after completion.

**3. Savings discovery rate**
Definition: Audits showing > $100/month savings ÷ total audits
Target: > 50%
Why it matters: If most audits show $0 savings, the tool is not
surfacing real value — either the pricing data is wrong or the
audit logic needs improvement.

## What to Instrument First

1. Page load → audit completion (funnel drop-off)
2. Audit completion → email submitted (lead conversion)
3. Email submitted → shareable link clicked (viral coefficient)
4. Savings amount distribution (are we finding real savings?)
5. Which tools are most commonly added (product insight)

## Pivot Trigger

**If email capture rate stays below 10% after 500 audit completions**
— the value proposition is not landing. Either:
- The savings numbers are too low to motivate action
- The email ask is too early or too aggressive
- The tool is attracting the wrong audience

At that point: run user interviews, test moving email capture earlier
or later, or test a different CTA ("get your report" vs "save my audit").

## What Success Looks Like at 30 Days

- 500+ audit completions
- 100+ email captures
- 20+ shareable links generated
- 5+ Credex consultation requests
- Organic shares driving > 30% of traffic
