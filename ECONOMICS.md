# Economics

## What is a Converted Lead Worth to Credex?

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Assumptions:
- Average credit purchase: $500
- Gross margin on credits: ~20% = $100 per transaction
- Average customer buys credits 3x per year = $300 LTV
- With 10% churn adjustment: ~$270 LTV per converted customer

**One converted lead = ~$270 LTV (conservative)**

## CAC at Each Channel

| Channel | Est. Leads/month | Conversion to purchase | CAC |
|---------|-----------------|----------------------|-----|
| HN / Reddit organic | 50 | 3% | $0 (time only) |
| Twitter/X organic | 30 | 2% | $0 (time only) |
| Credex email list | 40 | 8% | $0 (existing list) |
| Direct DMs | 20 | 5% | $0 (time only) |

All channels are $0 paid CAC at launch. Time cost only.

## Conversion Funnel
1000 visitors/month
→ 400 audit completions (40% completion rate)
→ 80 email captures (20% of completions)
→ 16 consultation bookings (20% of captures)
→ 5 credit purchases (30% of consultations)

**5 purchases × $270 LTV = $1,350 revenue per month per 1000 visitors**

## What Makes This Profitable

Break-even is essentially day 1 — there is no paid CAC. The tool costs:
- Vercel hosting: $0 (free tier)
- Supabase: $0 (free tier)
- Anthropic API: ~$0.01 per audit summary
- Domain: ~$12/year

**Total monthly cost: < $20**

First credit purchase covers all infrastructure costs for the year.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
$1,000,000 ARR ÷ $300 avg annual spend per customer = 3,334 customers
Month 1-3:   100 audits/month → 2 purchases/month
Month 4-6:   500 audits/month → 10 purchases/month
Month 7-12:  2000 audits/month → 40 purchases/month
Month 13-18: 5000 audits/month → 100 purchases/month

**What has to be true:**
1. Audit completion rate stays above 35%
2. Email capture rate stays above 15%
3. Consultation → purchase rate reaches 30%
4. Organic sharing drives compounding traffic (shareable URL)
5. Credex closes deals efficiently once leads are warm

At 100 purchases/month × $300 LTV = $30,000 MRR = $360,000 ARR by month 18.
To hit $1M ARR, paid acquisition or partnerships would be needed in month 12+.

## North Star

**Revenue generated for Credex per month from tool-sourced leads.**

Not audit completions, not email captures — actual revenue. Everything else
is a leading indicator.
